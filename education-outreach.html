Education & Outreach

Project Battambang stands by the wholehearted belief that education is a lifelong process, and a major cornerstone of improving universal standards of living. Quality education enables individuals to acquire the knowledge and proficiencies needed for higher-skilled occupations that provide better income and self-sufficiency. Every child deserves the opportunity to receive quality education. The ability to read and write will lay a foundation for a future without poverty or hunger. Education equips children with fundamental knowledge and skills to better provide for themselves and empowers the community to improve health and prevent diseases like HIV.

Light A Dream Scholarship (LADS)

The Light-A-Dream Scholarship (LADS) is a long standing initiative that invests in the education of a group of selected students with the hope that these individuals will someday contribute back to the community and its betterment. Each year, approximately 30 scholars ranging from Grades 7 through 12 and even up to university, are carefully selected by the sisters of the MSSHJ based on their personal and financial needs, and are financially supported via the scholarship.

School Support Programme

Our School Support Programme focuses on physical, emotional, and psychological well-being. Delivered across partner primary schools and communities, lessons cover personal hygiene, nutrition, chronic disease prevention, mental health awareness, and school hygiene infrastructure.

Post-Screening Health Education

Post-screening health education and practical first aid workshops equip villagers and students with essential skills to handle minor injuries, bites, basic wound care, and preventive health practices in daily rural life.
