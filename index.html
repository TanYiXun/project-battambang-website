Our Initiatives

WHO WE ARE

Project Battambang is an overseas community service project organized by students from the Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine, National University of Singapore. Since 2012, our team has been dedicated to improving healthcare access, providing health education, and supporting education initiatives for underserved communities in Battambang and Poipet, Cambodia.

OUR KEY INITIATIVES

Sustainable community health screenings, education scholarships, and clinical referral pathways in Cambodia.

Health Screening

Providing mass village health clinics, door-to-door screenings, chronic disease monitoring, and hospital referrals in Poipet.

Learn More

Education Development

Investing in youth through the Light-A-Dream Scholarship, School Support Programme, and community hygiene education.
